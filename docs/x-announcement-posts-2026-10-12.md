# Official X announcement posts — October 12, 2026

Claude and Gemini were prioritized for this pass. Grok CLI located candidate public posts; their official account, visible post content, and relation to the recorded release were then checked directly on X. Search snippets alone were not treated as verification.

Coverage: **27/27 Claude records** and **21/23 Gemini records** have exact official post links. Existing verified posts and a recent-release pass also supply links for selected records from other labs; those historical catalogs have not received the same complete X audit.

The `announcement_x_url` field is independent of the selected date basis. Cards and release details open it in a new tab. A missing verified post leaves the card icon disabled. Only an X announcement event can supply a fallback; availability events, generic profiles, search pages, benchmark replies, and later promotions cannot.

Shared posts are used only when they explicitly name the variants. Claude Mythos 5 uses the same-day launch-thread post that names its restricted release; the family thread root only introduces Fable 5. Gemini 3 Pro similarly uses the launch-thread post naming Pro. Gemini Ultra 1.0 links the February 8 Gemini Advanced release announcement, the event represented by its availability-only record. These links do not change previously documented dates or event precision. X timestamps may fall on the next calendar day in UTC+8 compared with a date-only official article.
